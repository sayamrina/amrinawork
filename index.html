What I can build for you

Six kinds of work, often combined. Each project starts small, with a working demo on your own data.

  • AI assistants and chatbots

    A chat assistant for your website, WhatsApp or internal team. It answers from your own documents, such as price lists, policies, menus or course material, and shows the source. When it is not sure, it passes the conversation to a person.

    For example: Guest questions for hotels and restaurants, student support for courses, internal policy questions

  • AI automation

    Language models connected to the tools you already use. E-mails, forms and PDFs become checked data, reply drafts and short reports. Prices, rules and calculations stay in normal code, so the numbers are right.

    For example: Reply drafts for guest e-mails, data from invoices and forms, ticket sorting in Jira or Teams

  • Websites and web apps

    Fast, mobile-first websites and redesigns that keep your brand, your content and your Google ranking, plus small web apps such as ordering or booking tools.

    For example: Hotel website redesign, multilingual company website, breakfast ordering by QR code

  • E-learning and learning platforms

    Moodle set-up and themes, interactive courses, AI tutors and learning analytics for schools, academies and company training.

    For example: Moodle with badges and certificates, H5P and Articulate courses, a course tutor built on your slides

  • Project management and internal systems

    Web tools for the way your team really works: requests and tickets, approval steps, task boards, priorities and deadlines, with roles and permissions. They connect to Jira, Trello or Microsoft Teams, or replace scattered spreadsheets and e-mail threads.

    For example: A support desk with ticket priorities and response times, an approval flow for new projects, a project tracker with a dashboard

  • Data dashboards and reports

    Dashboards that turn bookings, sales, course or support data into a few clear numbers, updated automatically, with short written summaries checked against the data.

    For example: Learning analytics in Moodle, website visitors with Google Analytics, Tableau dashboards for management

Questions clients ask

What does a project cost?

Every project is quoted after a short call, as a fixed price for the agreed scope. Running costs, such as hosting and AI usage per month, are estimated in the proposal so there are no surprises.

Is our data safe?

Yes, this is designed in from the start. Data can stay on EU servers, personal data can be removed before any AI call, and where needed the assistant runs on a self-hosted open model instead of an external service.

What if the assistant gives a wrong answer?

It answers only from your approved documents and shows the source, so your team can check it. When nothing matches, it says so and hands over to a person instead of guessing.

Will a new website hurt our Google ranking?

No, if it is done carefully. Existing addresses are kept or redirected, titles and descriptions are carried over, and Google Search Console is checked after launch.

Who owns the result?

You do. The code, content and accounts are handed over to you at the end of the project.
